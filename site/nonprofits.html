Sponsors & the field

Sponsors pay for the day. Their teams are in the field.

  • Tiers from Title to Hole-in-One, each with what it includes; a package with a foursome puts a team in the field at once, TBD seats counted as players
  • Pledged, received and outstanding per sponsor; every payment recorded
  • Teams sold whole: open seats count so the caterer’s number is right
  • Sponsor contacts on the donor list, ready for next year
Registration & the golf day

Everything the golf page does, for the charity scramble.

The public registration page, shotgun groups, printed cards with QR codes, live scoring from the carts, the leaderboard on the clubhouse TV and GameCast at the reception. Places get donated gifts, never payouts. The results sheet is ready for the awards.

The golf, screen by screen
The gala

Tickets, tables, paddles, and an auction on every phone.

  • Tickets with price and fair market value, tables, a guest list with a paddle for everyone
  • RSVPs from the event page land on the list; join with a name and phone to bid
  • The silent auction: opening bid, buy now, minimum raise, a close time, reserves the bidders never see
  • The live auction sold by paddle; paddle-raise donations
  • My bids on every guest’s phone: leading, outbid, won
Raffles & drawings

Drawn the way an auditor would want.

  • Tickets numbered in order of sale by paddle; the 50/50 on the golf day the same way
  • Lock the list before a draw: a fingerprint of every ticket; the draw refuses a list that changed
  • The winner is recorded before the reveal; winners leave the drum
  • Voids and resets need a reason and are logged
  • The official record PDF: tickets, buyers, the fingerprint, every draw
The cashier

Checkout from a phone at the door

A cashier link lists every paddle with what it owes: tickets, silent wins, lots, raffle tickets, gifts. Take a payment by card, cash, check or Zelle, record a live sale or a gift. The hub takes each action into the event once, then the event is the record.

The acknowledgement letter: what they paid, the fair market value received, the amount that may be deductible, the tax ID, the thank-you line. E-mailed from the organization’s own address.

Budget & treasury

Budget every event. See the year.

  • Each event has a plan per stream, tickets to donations, and one expense list with a budget and an actual per line
  • The golf tournament’s money rides in: entries, sponsors, the 50/50, its expenses
  • The annual budget by program; Budget vs actual as a PDF and a CSV for the board
  • A ledger with bank import; the treasurer’s report handed off clean
Your people

Donors & guests, users, point of sale.

  • The directory in your words: donors, guests and players, with city and known since
  • Users with titles, board member to volunteer, and a login each; an organization’s own e-mail sender
  • A register for event sales with a sales report, no restaurant machinery in the way
  • Anonymous surveys for the committee, the sponsors or the players
