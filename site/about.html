About

Built by the people who run club golf.

Who we are

It started as a budget for one tournament.

The people running a member tournament were done stitching the weekend together out of a tournament platform, a spreadsheet, a payment app and a legal pad. So they built the budget. Then the tournament. Then the season, and the whole club.

Now it runs other clubs. Every screen exists because a pro, a treasurer or a committee chair needed it on a Saturday morning.

Our mission

One place for a club to run its golf — and a hub of their own for every association and every group inside it, so the people who make club golf happen can spend their time on golf.
