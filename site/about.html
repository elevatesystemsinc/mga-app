About

Built by the people who run club golf.

Who we are

It started as a budget for one tournament.

The people running a member tournament were done stitching the weekend together out of a tournament platform, a spreadsheet, a payment app and a legal pad. So they built the budget. Then the tournament. Then the season, the associations and the Friday games, then the club’s accounts, registers and sheets, then the nonprofit’s gala.

Now it runs other clubs and organizations. Every screen exists because a pro, a treasurer, a server or a committee chair needed it on a Saturday morning, and nothing ships until it has survived a real event.

Our mission

One place for a club to run its golf, its operations and its fundraising, and a hub of their own for every association and every group inside it, so the people who make club golf happen can spend their time on golf.

01The club owns its directory.Always. One member record, club-wide; everyone else sees a view of it.
02A board’s money is the board’s.The club sees the calendar, not the ledger.
03If it takes a week to learn, it isn’t finished.Whoever is on shift can run it: the pro, the server, the volunteer at the door.
04Scoring is live and the math is exact.Every format, every side game, to the cent, net zero on every hole.
05Nothing is keyed twice.The field feeds the groups, the groups feed the cards and the phones, the phones feed the boards and the books.
06Your mark, not ours.Every card, statement, receipt and e-mail carries the club’s or the organization’s own logo.
