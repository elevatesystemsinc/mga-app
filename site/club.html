Member accounts & billing

The club’s billing system, not a bolt-on.

  • Membership classes with their monthly, quarterly and annual charges; proration for a mid-month join
  • Run monthly billing: dues, cart, locker, trail, capital, unspent food & beverage minimums
  • Run statements: finance charges and late fees posted, one branded PDF with a page per member, e-mailed
  • Aging current to 90+, FIFO settlement, a void with a reason, an adjustment that never edits the original
  • A GL export the accountant can take straight into the ledger
  • Association dues collected on the member’s club account and credited back
The account

Every charge with its receipt.

A running ledger with tax and service broken out. A register charge carries the register’s own ticket; a signed slip is a tap away. Adjust a charge with a reason and the difference posts as a credit; the original row stays. Statements and the e-mail of the latest one sit on the same page.

Member portal

Members see their account and book from their phone.

  • Sign in with a one-time e-mail link; no password to forget
  • Balance, activity, statements as PDFs, a signed slip behind a charge
  • Pay online or set up autopay through the club’s own Stripe account; Flagstick takes no fee
  • Book a tee time, a court, a cabana or a lesson under the rules the club sets
  • What’s on: the club’s public events
Online payments

Stripe Connect, the club’s account

The club connects its own Stripe account from Billing setup. Members pay by card or US bank account; each payment posts to the account once, refunds post back, autopay charges the balance due when statements run.

Rules, enforced

Bookings the database checks

Hours, the slot grid, how far ahead members may book, capacity, one a day, office-only facilities, closed times, suspended members, a facility fee posted to the account. The portal cannot bend them.

Point of sale

The grill knows the member. The statement knows the grill.

  • Restaurants, the golf shop and the racket shop as outlets, each with its products, categories and menus
  • Item libraries to start from: the club menu with modifiers, the shop with sizes, stringing with tension
  • Charge to account, cash or card; split a check evenly, by seat or by item, each portion its own tip and method
  • A mandatory service charge on every food & beverage check, prices all-in, no tip asked in the app
  • Tickets by day with a shift report per outlet: sales, tax, service, tips by server, voids, top items
  • Receipts on 80 mm thermal printers, black only, the club’s logo
On the floor

Registers, kitchen screens, servers with PINs.

  • A register station on any tablet: PIN lock, the floor with tables and tabs, the check, Send to the kitchen, Pay
  • A salad fires to one prep station and a steak to another; drinks rung at the bar fire nowhere; courses fire in order
  • Kitchen expo screens that update the moment a course is sent; bump and recall
  • Servers see their own tables; a manager’s PIN voids, transfers, reopens or leaves the station
  • Every check gets a check number with a check digit; the printed slip carries a QR; the daily close-out scans the signed slips, reads the tips and locks the day
Tickets & the shift

Closed, counted, reported.

Every ticket of the day by outlet, with the shift’s numbers: net sales, service, tax, tips by server, by payment type, voids. A closed check can be reopened from the station the same day and from the hub after. Slips out for a signature close with the tip written on them, and the tip goes to the server’s line for payroll.

Bookings

Every sheet in one place. Each department sees its own.

  • The tee sheet by course, tennis and pickleball courts, the pool and cabanas, lessons for every department
  • Tap a slot to book, check in, add guests, or charge a fee to the account
  • Block times by drag; close the course for a day or a range with the reason on the sheet
  • Portal bookings marked on the sheet; the printed sheet for the starter
  • The club calendar: every sheet, closure, event and shift, by department, on one month
Staff & schedule

Who works where, and when they were here.

  • One staff list across golf, racket sports, aquatics, food & beverage and the club, with departments and roles
  • The same PIN unlocks a register and clocks in
  • A week-at-a-time schedule builder: repeat a shift, copy last week, hours per person, print the week
  • A time clock kiosk per department and clock-in from a register’s lock screen
  • Who is on now, hours this week, a timesheet CSV for payroll
Reports

The year and the quarter, on one page.

Billed, net revenue, received and receivable at the period end. Revenue by billing item with tax and service, receipts by method, the registers by outlet, by month, against the year’s budget. A CSV for the spreadsheet and a branded PDF for the board.

The directory

One member record, club-wide.

Import the club’s roster once; member numbers, GHIN, e-mail and names matched, the rest created. The club owns the record. An association’s membership is a view of it; a group draws from it; billing classes and status sit on it. Staff see the whole club; a board login sees its board; an organizer link sees its group.
