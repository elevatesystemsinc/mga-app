Golf management

Everything a golf season needs.
Already connected.

The pro shop runs the club’s events. Every association and every regular game runs its own, inside the same system. Scoring is live on every phone, the math is exact for every format, and the results go straight to the books.

Tournaments

From sign-up to printed cards in one afternoon.

  • A public registration page: players find their name, add partners or ask the shop to pair them
  • Teams by hand, a roster import, or sponsor packages that carry a foursome, with TBD seats counted
  • Flights by handicap with a partner cap, whole groups kept together; pairings by standings on day two
  • Shotgun or tee times, one tap; every group gets a group ID
  • Sponsors, meals, budget, checklist, Calcutta and the 50/50 on the same event
  • A schedule that spans the reception the night before and the dinner after
Registration & check-in

The field fills itself.

The registration page shows the day as it will run: the schedule, how the format is scored, the handicap allowance, what the places win. Entries land in the field as teams. On the morning, the table checks players in from a phone, alphabetical and searchable, with every check-in showing up everywhere within seconds.

  • Names only on the public page, never e-mails or handicaps
  • A private check-in link for the table, no board password
  • Field questions, a note for the shop, open spots marked for pairing
Pairings & scorecards

Groups built, cards printed, codes on every one.

  • Build groups from the field or from the flights; teams stay together
  • Branded scorecards with the strokes dotted on the holes, signature lines and a QR that opens live scoring with the group already joined
  • One card per team or per group, two to a page for the cart
  • Groups stay editable mid-round; a corrected index reaches every phone
Live scoring

Phones in the carts. A board in the lounge.

  • Players join with a group ID or the QR on the card, or from the Flagstick app on iOS and Android
  • One score per team for a scramble, one per player otherwise; scores save offline and send when signal returns
  • Leaderboards to par, gross or net, by flight and by competition
  • A full-screen board for the clubhouse TV that refreshes itself
  • A name on any board opens the full scorecard; the committee corrects a card in place
GameCast

A page for the gallery.

Read-only, so it can never disturb a round. The course with every cart on the hole it is playing, a live feed of birdies, eagles and lead changes, a hole panel, the board per competition. Follow one player, or put it on the big screen at the reception. Courses with mapped hole lines draw on an aerial map.

Every format

Pick the game. The math is done.

  • Stroke, stableford, modified stableford, quota, par/bogey, match play, Nassau
  • Scramble, shamble, best ball, aggregate, alternate shot, greensome, split days
  • One, two or three balls counting, rotating patterns, every ball on 9 and 18
  • Handicap allowances by format, a partner cap, no pops on par 3s, adjustable points per score
  • Skins by pot or by hole, validation on the next hole, gross beats net
  • Results totalled across rounds, ranked overall and by flight, as a branded PDF

Course library

Twenty thousand courses. Yours on top.

Every card, tee box and rating in one library, searchable by course, club or city and browsable by country and region. A club’s own courses sit beside it, visible only to that club. A tournament at another course is one pick away: the round, the flights, the printed cards and the phones all read the right card.

  • Your defaults first everywhere a course is chosen
  • Paste a card in from the scorecard, with the tees, ratings and yardages checked
  • Hole lines for GameCast’s map where they are mapped
Associations

A hub for every board. Their own money.

  • Men’s, ladies’, senior: each with a schedule, board, season and treasury
  • Season budget against actuals, per-event P&L, bank import, the treasurer’s report as a PDF
  • Dues billed by the association, collected by the club, credited back
  • Board logins see their board, nothing else; boards invite their own members
  • Anonymous member surveys with results the board can act on
  • Handed to next year’s board intact
Member games

The Thursday game runs itself.

  • New game on a phone in three taps: who’s playing, what we’re playing, share the link
  • Any number of pots on one round: stroke play, skins, best ball, dots, a team Nassau, by hand
  • Blind-draw partners staged on screen; skins called out hole by hole after the round
  • The players close the round, draw and record the payouts from their phones
  • A season ledger of who is up, who owes, and the fewest payments to settle
  • An organizer link: no pro shop login needed, ever
Cart games

Wolf, hammer, banker, flip, the robin.

Five in a cart and no big game? Set up a cart game on the scoring page. The strip on every hole names the wolf, takes the pick, shows the sides’ best balls and settles the money net zero hole by hole. A second phone in the cart picks up the same game. Big Lou at 16. Birdies double, eagles triple. The hub’s game page shows the ledger when you are done.

Budget & treasury

The treasurer’s report writes itself.

  • A season budget by program: every tournament, every event, the organization’s own lines
  • Actuals as they land: entries, sponsor payments, the 50/50, the Calcutta, expenses
  • Budget vs actual on one page, as a PDF and a CSV
  • A ledger with bank import and reconciliation
  • One year filter in the rail moves the whole hub: 2027 on the rail is 2027 everywhere
