How this works

1

Five tabs at the bottom. Home is the dashboard. Sponsors tracks confirmed pledges. Outreach is everyone you're targeting. Budget is all the money. Event is the schedule and food & beverage.

2

Money comes in? On Sponsors, tap the gold Log payment button — full or partial, Zelle/Check/Cash. Rows turn gold when partially paid, green when paid in full, and every total updates. Tapped by mistake? Hit Undo.

3

Working a prospect? On Outreach, tap them for one-tap Call/Text/Email and a Log contact today button. When they commit, tap Add to Sponsors and they move over automatically.

4

Budgeting? Blue boxes are the numbers you enter — golfer count, entry fee, expense lines. Everything else calculates itself, including sponsor revenue which pulls live from the Sponsors tab.

5

Next year's tournament? Tap the year at the top → New year. Sponsors, prospects, budget structure, and schedule all carry over with the money reset to zero.
